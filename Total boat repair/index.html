Who We Are

Old Town's Marine Service Experts

Total Boat Repair & Sales is a locally owned marine service center at 25771 SE Highway 19 in Old Town, Florida, serving Suwannee County and the surrounding communities. Our team combines deep hands-on expertise with a genuine passion for boating to deliver service you can trust.

As an authorized Honda Marine dealer and service center, we have the factory training, tools, and genuine parts to service all makes and models — from small outboards to multi-engine rigs. Whether you need routine maintenance or complex engine diagnostics, we get it done right.
